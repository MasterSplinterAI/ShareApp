# Mobile video effects delay

## Symptom

User selects blur or background on Pre-join; after joining on mobile (especially iOS Safari), effect takes ~3–8 seconds to appear or briefly shows unprocessed video.

## Workaround

Wait a few seconds after join; or disable and re-enable the effect from the in-meeting camera menu.

## Reporting

Include device model, OS version, browser, effect type, and whether issue occurs on Pre-join only or also when changing in-meeting.
