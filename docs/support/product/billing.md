# Billing

Organizations subscribe to Lalia plans with usage limits for meetings, translation minutes, and seats.

## Plans and upgrades

Org admins manage billing from **Settings → Organization**. Upgrades take effect after payment confirmation.

## Invoices and payment failures

Failed payments may restrict new meetings until resolved. Admins receive email notices when billing status changes.

## Refunds and disputes

Billing disputes require human review — do not promise refunds in automated replies. Escalate to ops via Telegram approval flow.

## Usage overages

Some plans allow overage billing; see your org dashboard usage tab for current period consumption.
