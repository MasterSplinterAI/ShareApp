# Billing & plans

Org admins manage billing from **Settings → Billing** in the left sidebar.

## Plans overview

| Plan | Typical use |
|------|-------------|
| **Free** | Personal use, limited meeting and translation minutes per month |
| **Starter** | Small teams, higher included minutes |
| **Pro** | Business workspace, team features, higher limits |

Exact limits depend on the user's current plan — always check userContext.plan when answering (included meeting/translation minutes, team workspace flag).

## Who can change billing

Only **workspace owners** and org admins can upgrade or manage payment methods. Regular members should ask their workspace owner.

## Usage limits

When users hit monthly limits, they may need to upgrade or wait until the next billing cycle. Usage counters appear in Settings for admins.

## Complimentary accounts

Some workspaces have complimentary (comp) access with unlimited usage — userContext.plan.isComp indicates this.

## Billing disputes

Escalate refund or chargeback requests to human support — do not promise refunds in automated replies.
