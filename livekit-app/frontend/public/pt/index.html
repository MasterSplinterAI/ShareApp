Something else on your mind?

Let’s talk
How does live translation work?

You speak in your own language. Lalia translates the speech out loud into the other person’s chosen language, with live bilingual captions so you can both read along. Each participant chooses their own language.

Is Lalia for personal calls or team meetings?

Both. Ring a saved contact for a one-on-one conversation, share a guest link, or host a group meeting. Lalia is built for individuals as well as teams working across languages.

How many languages can I use?

Lalia supports live captions and translation in 30+ languages. Create a free account to explore the language options for your conversation.

Can I try it for free?

Yes. The Free plan includes 60 participant-minutes and 60 translation minutes each month. No credit card is required to get started. Each person’s time counts toward participant-minutes.

What happens after the call?

Use the bilingual transcript to generate an executive summary, action items, or a decisions report. You can email the report and transcript to participants, contacts, or other recipients, and export PDF, Markdown, or text.

What happens if I reach my plan limit?

Free stops at 60 minutes on each meter. Starter and Pro offer optional overage charges with a hard stop at twice the included minutes on both meters. Participant-minutes and translation minutes are tracked separately.
