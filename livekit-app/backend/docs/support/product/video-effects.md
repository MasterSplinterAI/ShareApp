# Video effects

Video effects (blur, virtual backgrounds, etc.) are applied on the Pre-join screen and in-meeting from the camera menu.

## Pre-join vs in-meeting

Effects selected on Pre-join should carry into the room. On some mobile browsers there may be a short delay before the effect appears after join — see troubleshooting/effects-mobile.md.

## Changing effects in a meeting

Use the camera chevron / settings menu on the control bar to change blur or background without leaving the room.

## Performance

Heavy backgrounds may reduce frame rate on older devices. Try blur-only or disable effects if video stutters.
